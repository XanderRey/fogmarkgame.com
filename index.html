Every square of the map is a picture-logic puzzle. Solve one and the fog lifts on a hand-painted pixel island you can walk across: beaches, pine woods, villages, and rare trees nobody else will ever find.

A map made of puzzles

Never guess

Every puzzle can be solved by logic alone, and neighbouring squares share edges, so each one you solve hands clues to the next.

An island of your own

Every tree, house and rock is rolled at random. A few are Rare, Epic, Legendary or Mythic, and they're yours, with the day you found them.

Three kinds of island

Temperate meadows, tropic lagoons and snowy frost isles: 900 islands from quick 5×5 tiles to 25×25 evenings.

Your home cove

Build benches, a windmill, a jetty and more from what you gather, and grow your own little island.

A puzzle a day

One daily picture, the same for everyone. Keep your flame lit and compare times with friends.

Play your way

Mouse, keyboard, controller or touch. No time limits, no pop-up ads, and updates with new islands are free.
